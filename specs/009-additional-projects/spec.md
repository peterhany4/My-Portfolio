# Feature Specification: 009 — Additional Projects

## Overview

Provide evidence of breadth without overwhelming the visitor, featuring projects like the Football Team and Player Management System, Password Manager, and Electoral Voting Simulator through progressive disclosure that keeps featured projects visually dominant.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-10
- Q: Are there any ambiguities regarding additional project selection or visual hierarchy? → A: None. Additional projects are presented with secondary visual weight to ensure featured projects remain dominant.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### Scenario 1: Exploring Additional Technical Breadth
- **Actor**: Site Visitor / Recruiter
- **Description**: A visitor reviews additional projects to see breadth across systems, desktop, and algorithms.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Additional projects present what was built, why it exists, and technologies used.
  - Featured projects remain visually dominant, while additional projects support breadth without clutter.

## Functional Requirements

### FR1: Additional Project Selection & Content
- The Additional Projects section MUST showcase projects such as the Football Team and Player Management System, Password Manager, and Electoral Voting Simulator.
- Each project MUST explain what it is, why it exists, and technologies used.

### FR2: Progressive Disclosure & Hierarchy
- Additional projects MUST maintain secondary visual weight compared to featured projects.

## Success Criteria

- **Measurable & Verifiable Outcomes**:
  - Additional projects demonstrate technical breadth without distracting from primary featured evidence.

## Assumptions & Constraints
- Additional projects follow Featured Projects in the single-page scroll layout.
