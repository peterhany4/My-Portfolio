# Research: Current Focus / Learning Section (Feature 006)

## 1. Progression Presentation
- **Decision**: Card-based visual grouping with explicit status badges ("Current", "Exploring", "Learning") rather than numeric skill bars.
- **Rationale**: Adheres strictly to Constitution Rule II & V (avoiding misleading skill-bar gimmicks and communicating growth honestly).
- **Alternatives Considered**: Percentage progress bars (explicitly prohibited by Constitution).
