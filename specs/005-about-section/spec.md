# Feature Specification: 005 — About Section

## Overview

Provide concise, accurate context about Peter Hany as a Computer Science student and software developer, explaining his development background, learning philosophy, and technical direction without duplicating the CV or exaggerating experience.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-10
- Q: Are there any ambiguities regarding student background or development experience in the About section? → A: None. Content strictly adheres to authentic representation and project Constitution principles.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### Scenario 1: Understanding the Developer's Background and Philosophy
- **Actor**: Site Visitor / Recruiter
- **Description**: A visitor reads the About section to understand Peter's background as a Computer Science student, his developer journey, and his continuous learning philosophy.
- **Acceptance Criteria':**
  - Section clearly communicates student status and genuine software development background.
  - Learning philosophy emphasizes continuous building, learning, and exploration.
  - Content avoids résumé duplication and unverified claims.

## Functional Requirements

### FR1: Student & Development Background
- The About section MUST present Peter's current university Computer Science status accurately.
- The section MUST explain software development experience honestly without exaggeration or claims of unearned seniority.

### FR2: Learning Philosophy & Technical Direction
- The section MUST communicate a commitment to continuous learning and exploring new areas of software engineering.
- The copy MUST remain concise, human-sounding, and easy to update.

## Success Criteria

- **Measurable & Verifiable Outcomes**:
  - Visitors understand Peter's background and development direction within 10 seconds of reading.
  - Content successfully passes the authenticity and accuracy verification against project constraints.

## Assumptions & Constraints
- The About section follows the Hero section in the single-page scroll sequence.
- Content is structured to remain maintainable as Peter's education and experience evolve.
