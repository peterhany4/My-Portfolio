# Data Model: 018 — Content and Privacy Audit

## Data Integrity Rules
- **Source of Truth**: GitHub repositories for technical/project data; verified academic CV for formal education and achievements.
- **Privacy Compliance**: Proprietary projects (e.g., Cashier App) must be represented via sanitized case studies or feature descriptions without exposing internal business logic, DB schemas, or credentials.
- **Claim Integrity**: Elimination of inflated metrics or unverified commercial claims.
