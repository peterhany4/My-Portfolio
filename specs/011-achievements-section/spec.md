# Feature Specification: 011 — Achievements Section

## Overview

Show evidence of activity and accomplishments outside ordinary project listings (such as DeepX Hackathon, DEPI recognition, training hours, and technical competitions) without turning the portfolio into an uncurated certificate gallery.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-10
- Q: Are there any ambiguities regarding achievement selection or verification? → A: None. Only achievements supported by verified reliable information are included.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### Scenario 1: Reviewing Technical Accomplishments and Recognition
- **Actor**: Site Visitor / Recruiter
- **Description**: A visitor reviews achievements to see recognition of technical ability, consistency, and competition success.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Achievements highlight technical ability, consistency, training, and competition participation.
  - Layout is concise and supports the overall portfolio story rather than dominating it.

## Functional Requirements

### FR1: Achievement Selection & Presentation
- The Achievements section MUST present accomplishments such as the DeepX Hackathon, DEPI recognition, and training hours supported by verified information.
- The section MUST prioritize meaningful achievements demonstrating technical ability, consistency, and learning.
- The layout MUST remain concise, avoiding an overwhelming certificate gallery.

## Success Criteria

- **Measurable & Verifiable Outcomes**:
  - Achievements are clear, credible, and verifiable within 10 seconds.
  - Zero inflated or unverified claims exist.

## Assumptions & Constraints
- Achievements follow Education in the single-page scroll layout.
