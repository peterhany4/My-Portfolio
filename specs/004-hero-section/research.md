# Research: Hero Section (Feature 004)

## 1. Hero Composition & Typography
- **Decision**: Large bold heading (`<h1>`), prominent subtitle badge for developer identity, and concise supporting paragraph.
- **Rationale**: Immediately captures visitor attention and communicates identity within the critical 3-second window.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Centered minimalist text only (lacks visual impact).

## 2. CTA Navigation & Anchor Links
- **Decision**: Primary button linked to `#projects` with smooth scroll behavior.
- **Rationale**: Directs visitors immediately to evidence of actual software development ability (Projects are Evidence).
- **Alternatives Considered**: Multi-page routing (violates single-page portfolio requirements).
