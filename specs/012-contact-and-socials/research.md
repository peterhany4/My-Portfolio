# Research: Contact and Social Links (Feature 012)

## 1. Contact Presentation
- **Decision**: Prominent CTA card featuring direct email action, GitHub, LinkedIn, and CV download link with secure external attributes.
- **Rationale**: Maximizes conversion for recruiters and visitors wishing to get in touch.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Complex contact form with backend validation (violates Purposeful Simplicity and Non-Goals).
